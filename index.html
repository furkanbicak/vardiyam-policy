Privacy Policy for Vardiyam

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Vardiyam ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Ahmet Furkan Bıçak, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app processes, how that information is used, and the choices available to you when using the app.

1. Information We Process

Vardiyam is designed to help police officers, watchmen, gendarmerie personnel, and private security staff manage personal shift schedules, duties, leave, and employment-related records.

Depending on how you use the app, the following information may be processed:

  • Profile information you enter, such as your name, profession, rank, duty location, date of birth, service start date, and service or retirement calculation preferences
  • Shift patterns and history, daily schedule changes, duties, events, notes, and leave records
  • Your selected city or district and its center coordinates for weather forecasts
  • Reminder settings, local notification identifiers, and the last backup time

2. How Your Data Is Stored

Your profile, shifts, duties, events, notes, leave records, and app settings are stored locally on your device. No user account is required.

We do not operate a backend that receives or stores these records during normal app use, and we cannot remotely access the records on your device.

3. Weather and Open-Meteo

Vardiyam uses Open-Meteo to provide weather forecasts for your selected location.

When weather-related features are used, the app may send:

  • the district name you select, to find its coordinates
  • the latitude and longitude of the selected place's center, to request a weather forecast

The app does not request your device's GPS location or track your location in the background. Your name, birth date, shift times, notes, and leave records are not included in weather requests.

As part of an internet connection, Open-Meteo may receive your IP address and technical request information. Its server logs may include geographical coordinates and are handled under its own retention and privacy practices.

Open-Meteo's privacy practices are described in its own documentation:

  • https://open-meteo.com/en/terms

4. How We Use Information

We use processed information to:

  • provide the core functionality of the app
  • save and display your shift schedules, duties, events, notes, and leave records
  • display informational service and retirement calculations
  • show weather forecasts for your selected city or district
  • schedule local shift and event reminders when you grant notification permission
  • export or restore backup files when you request it

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; no remote notification server is used. Notification titles and contents may appear on your lock screen depending on your device settings. Core shift and record features work offline; weather requires an internet connection.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We do not share your profile, shift schedules, duties, notes, or leave records with advertisers.

Limited location and technical information may be processed by Open-Meteo as described above.

When you use the backup feature, the app exports your profile, shifts, duties, notes, leave records, and settings to a JSON file. You choose where to save or share it, such as Files, iCloud, Google Drive, email, or another destination. Your chosen provider may process the file under its own terms. The app does not add password protection or encryption to exported backups.

When restoring a backup, the app reads the file you select through the system file picker; it does not automatically scan your files.

6. Tracking and Advertising

Vardiyam does not include advertising, user tracking, analytics SDKs, or in-app purchases in the current app version.

7. Data Retention

App records generally remain on your device until you delete them, reset the app's data, or use your operating system's option to uninstall the app and delete its data.

Resetting or uninstalling the app does not delete exported backup files or separate cloud or device backups. These must be managed and deleted separately. Operating-system backup and restore features depend on your device settings and the provider's terms.

Technical records processed by third-party services may be retained according to their own privacy and operational policies.

8. Your Choices

You can:

  • edit your profile information inside the app
  • delete your notes and records
  • reset app records using Özlük → Ayarlar → Tüm verileri sıfırla (Profile → Settings → Reset all data)
  • manage notification permissions and lock-screen visibility through device settings
  • choose whether and where to save or share a backup
  • delete exported files and any cloud or device backups from their respective locations
  • uninstall the app using the option that also removes app data

There is no server-side user account or copy of your app records for us to delete remotely.

9. Children's Privacy

Vardiyam is intended for working adults and is not directed at children. If you believe information has been provided inappropriately through a support channel, please contact us using the contact options below without posting sensitive details publicly.

10. Security

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your device protected and your backup files secure, and share backups only with people you trust.

Do not enter confidential institutional information or other people's sensitive information into the app.

Vardiyam is not an official government app and is not affiliated with the Turkish General Directorate of Security, Gendarmerie General Command, or any other institution. Leave, retirement, and calendar calculations are informational and do not establish official entitlements or decisions.

11. International Use

Weather requests and files you choose to share may be processed by third-party providers in other jurisdictions under their own privacy terms.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. No advertising, analytics scripts, or cookies have been added to the page. GitHub may process technical visitor information, such as IP addresses, to serve the page under its own Privacy Statement:

  • https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published at this address with a revised effective date.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or need support for Vardiyam, you can contact the developer, Ahmet Furkan Bıçak, through the developer contact details on the app's product page or open a GitHub support issue:

  • https://github.com/furkanbicak/vardiyam-policy/issues/new

GitHub issues require a GitHub account and are public. Do not post backups, names, duty locations, shift schedules, or other personal information. Redact personal details from screenshots. You may include the app version, device model, and operating-system version to help describe a problem.

Information you voluntarily submit for support is processed through the chosen contact channel to respond to your request.
